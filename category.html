Har listing mein price, size (marla/kanal) aur location ki maloomat hoti hai, taake aap jaldi compare kar sakein.

Kaunsi Property Types Milti Hain

  • Residential Plots — DHA, Bahria Town, aur doosri housing societies
  • Houses/Villas — sale aur rent dono ke liye
  • Commercial — shops, offices, plazas
  • Agricultural Land — chhote shehron aur gaon ke areas se

Kharidne/Rent Se Pehle Ye Zaroor Karein

Kisi bhi housing society mein plot ya file kharidne se pehle uska CDA/RDA official status Verification page se confirm kar lein — is se lakhon rupay ke nuqsan se bacha ja sakta hai. Rent par lete waqt agreement likhwana aur advance/security deposit ki receipt lena bhi zaroori hai.

Pakistan Jobs Classified Ads — Government & Private

Jobs category mein government aur private vacancies newspaper classifieds se milti hain — Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi jaisay shehron ke liye. Government jobs mein qualification, department aur closing date, aur private jobs mein salary range aur location ki maloomat hoti hai.

Job Categories

  • Government/Public Sector — departments, autonomous bodies
  • Private Sector — corporate, retail, healthcare, education
  • Overseas/Visa-based Employment — Gulf, Europe destinations

Job Offer Verify Karein

Agar koi overseas job offer mile aur visa agency involve ho, to Scam Checker ya Verification page se agency ka BEOE record check karein — paisay dene se pehle. Ghair-license agencies aksar "guaranteed visa" ka jhoota dawa karti hain.

Application Ke Tips

Government job ads mein closing date se pehle apply karein aur required documents poore rakhein. Private job ads mein salary range aur location clearly diya hota hai — interview call aane par company ka naam Scam Checker se verify karna faidamand hai.

Government jobs kaise mil sakti hain?

Newspaper classifieds se extract ki gayi government vacancy ads yahan browse kar sakte hain.

Job offer fake hone ka khatra kaise kam karein?

Scam Checker se agency/company ka naam check karein, aur advance payment se pehle ehtiyat barten.

Kya main khud job vacancy post kar sakta hoon?

Haan, Submit Ad page se recruiter/employer apni vacancy free post kar sakte hain.

Overseas job ke liye kya check karna zaroori hai?

Visa agency ka BEOE license number aur offer ki details Verification page se confirm karein.
