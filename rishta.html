Newspaper Rishta Vs Online Matrimonial Sites — Farq Kya Hai

Rishta Board Use Karne Ka Tareeqa

  1. Tab select karein — Larkon ke Rishtay ya Larkiyon ke Rishtay.
  2. Shehar filter karein (optional) — Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Multan, Peshawar ya Quetta.
  3. Card ko swipe karein ya buttons se "Agla" (skip) ya "Interest Bhejo" select karein.
  4. Interest bhejne par WhatsApp khulta hai — pre-filled message ke sath, aap khud apna number likh kar bhej sakte hain.

Safety Tips

  • Kabhi bhi advance payment ya "processing fee" na dein — rishta match karna hamesha free hai.
  • Family ko involve karein — interest bhejne se pehle ghar walon se mashwara karein.
  • Pehli baat mein hi zyada personal maloomat share karne se parhez karein.

Privacy & Trust

Aapka number kabhi public nahi hota. Sirf jab dono taraf interest ho, connection WhatsApp ke zariye hota hai — mutual razamandi se, koi teesra platform involve nahi hota.

Rishta Se Pehle Biodata Bana Lein

Jab kisi profile ko interest bhej kar baat aage barhti hai, to family ko formal tareeqe se ek biodata dikhana rishta process ko zyada serious aur respectful banata hai. Agar aap ke paas abhi tak biodata nahi hai, to ToolNest ka free Rishta Biodata Maker istemal kar ke chand minute mein professional, print-ready biodata bana sakte hain — koi design skill zaroori nahi.

Larkon Aur Larkiyon Ke Rishtay — Dono Tabs Mein Farq

Larkon Ke Rishtay tab mein wo profiles hain jo shaadi ke liye ladka dhoond rahi hain, aur Larkiyon Ke Rishtay tab mein wo jo ladki dhoond rahi hain. Dono tabs mein filter aur interest-bhejne ka tareeqa bilkul same hai — sirf profile type alag hai. Family members chahen to dono tabs khol kar screen ek dosray ko dikha kar mashwara bhi kar sakte hain.
