Free Classified Ad Kaise Post Karein — Har Category Ka Guide

RoznamaAds.pk par Property, Jobs, Vehicles, Matrimonial, Visa aur doosri categories mein apni ad bilkul free post karein — Pakistan bhar ke readers tak pohanchein, koi sign-up zaroori nahi. Neeche har category ke liye ek chhota guide diya hai ke kya-kya likhna behtar hota hai.

Category Guide

CategoryKya Zaroor Likhein
PropertyPrice, size (marla/kanal), exact location/area
VehiclesModel, year, price, condition
JobsSalary range, qualification, job location
MatrimonialAge, profession — personal details bilkul rakhein privacy ka khayal
VisaDestination country, visa type
TendersDepartment, submission deadline

Ad Post Karne Ke Steps

  1. Category aur shehar select karein — Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad ya koi bhi shehar (ya "Other" likh kar apna shehar likhein).
  2. Category-specific fields fill karein (upar table dekhein).
  3. Clear description likhein aur contact number dein.
  4. Up to 5 photos add karein (har ek 5MB se kam).
  5. "Submit for Review" dabayein — admin review ke baad ad live ho jayegi.

Behtar Ad Likhne Ke Tips

  • Title mein hi price/key detail mention karein — jaise "10 Marla Plot — DHA Phase 6, 1.85 Crore".
  • Description mein saaf, sachi maloomat likhein — exaggeration se ad ka trust kam hota hai.
  • Achi quality, roshan photos use karein — blurry ya purani photos avoid karein.

Privacy & Trust

Aapka phone number website par masked format mein dikhta hai — kabhi seedha public nahi hota, sirf "Reveal Number" button dabane par visible hota hai. Har ad admin review se guzar kar hi live hoti hai, spam/fake ads filter ki jati hain.

Ad Approval Ke Criteria

Admin review mein ye dekha jata hai ke ad ki details complete hain, photos clear aur relevant hain, aur description mein koi misleading ya spam content nahi hai. Property ads mein price/location, vehicle ads mein model/year, aur job ads mein salary/qualification ka hona zaroori samjha jata hai — is se ad jaldi approve hoti hai.

Expired Ad Ko Dobara Post Karna

30 din baad ad automatically expire ho jati hai — agar item/vacancy/rishta abhi bhi available hai, to same details ke sath dobara "Submit for Review" kar dein, chhoti si update (jaisay price adjust) ke sath ad zyada fresh aur relevant lagti hai.

Category Change Ya Delete Kaise Karein

Agar submit ki gayi ad mein galat category select ho gayi ho, ya item bik/rent ho jane ke baad ad remove karni ho, to Contact page se "Ad Submission Help" subject select kar ke ad ki details (title, city, phone number) bhej dein — team update ya remove kar degi.
