🛂 Apna Visa Permission Number Verify Karein

Agar aapko koi visa/job offer mila hai, to BEOE ke official record se confirm karein: kya ye offer wahi agency de rahi hai jo claim kar rahi hai, kya salary sahi hai, aur kya offer abhi valid hai — paisay dene se pehle.

Ye button aapko seedha BEOE ki official website (beoe.gov.pk) ke us record ke page par le jayega — hum khud data nahi dikhate, taake aapko hamesha sab se up-to-date aur official jawab mile.

Paisay Dene Se Pehle Verify Karein — 9 Categories Mein Se Koi Bhi

Pakistan mein fraud sirf housing societies aur visa agencies tak mehdood nahi — fake job offers, ghost institutes, shell companies, fraudulent tenders, fake auctions, scam phone numbers aur phishing websites bhi aam hain. RoznamaAds.pk ka Scam & Risk Checker 9 mukhtalif categories cover karta hai, taake paisay ya waqt invest karne se pehle aap official aur community signals dono dekh sakein.

Result Kaise Samjhein

Har result ek "risk signal summary" hai — official record match, mukhtalif reports, aur RoznamaAds ki apni ad-history combine karke ek status label deta hai (jaise "Caution" ya "Warning"). Ye final legal faisla nahi, sirf ek starting point hai apni khud ki tehqeeq ke liye.

Phone aur Website Checks Khaas Kyun Hain

Phone number check karne se caller ki koi private/telecom data nahi milti — sirf ye dikhaya jata hai ke us number se RoznamaAds par kitni ads post hui hain aur kis naam se (multiple naam ek red flag ho sakta hai). Website check server-side HTTPS aur redirect behaviour test karta hai, taake fake/cloned websites ka pata chal sake jo asli company ka naam istemal karti hain.

Housing Society, Visa/OEP Agency, Job/Recruiter, Institute, Company, Tender/Supplier, Auction, Phone Number, aur Website/URL.
