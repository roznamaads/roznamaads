Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Overview

RoznamaAds.pk Pakistani newspaper classified ads ko digital, searchable format mein la kar users ke liye accessible banata hai. Ye Privacy Policy batati hai ke hum kaunsi maloomat collect karte hain aur unhe kaise handle karte hain.

2. Maloomat Jo Collect Hoti Hai

Jab aap "Submit Ad" page se apni ad post karte hain, hum aapka naam (optional), contact number, city, aur ad se related tafseel collect karte hain. Newspaper se extract ki gayi ads ke liye, hum sirf wo maloomat rakhte hain jo asal advertisement mein publicly print hui thi.

3. Contact Number Privacy

User safety ke liye, phone numbers website par masked format mein dikhaye jate hain (e.g. 0300-XXX-1234). Poora number sirf "Reveal Number" button click karne par dikhta hai — is se automated scraping aur spam calls kam hoti hain.

4. Newspaper Content Ka Istemal

Hum newspaper classifieds ka raw wording ya images copy nahi karte. AI ke zariye sirf structured data (category, price, location, contact) extract kiya jata hai aur naya, original wording generate hota hai — is se copyright risk kam hota hai.

5. WhatsApp Distribution

Ads sirf un WhatsApp groups mein share hoti hain jinhein users ne khud opt-in kar ke join kiya ho. Hum kisi bhi number par unsolicited bulk messaging nahi karte, jo Pakistan Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act (PECA) ke mutabiq hai.

6. Cookies & Analytics

Website performance samajhne ke liye hum Google Analytics jaisi basic analytics tools use kar sakte hain. Ye aapki personal identity track nahi karti.

7. Third-Party Links

Hamari website par kabhi kabhi ToolNest.link jaise related tools ke links honge. In external websites ki apni privacy policies hoti hain — hum unke content ke zimmedar nahi.

8. Ad Reporting & Moderation

Agar koi ad fake ya scam lage, "Report this ad" button se aap admin ko inform kar sakte hain. Reported ads ka review admin panel se manually hota hai.

9. Contact Us

Privacy se related kisi bhi sawal ke liye, hamein Contact page ke zariye message karein.
