About RoznamaAds.pk

Hamara Mission

Pakistan mein har din Jang, Express, Nawa-i-Waqt, Dawn jaise newspapers hazaron classified ads publish karte hain — property, jobs, vehicles, matrimonial, visa, auctions aur bohot kuch. Lekin ye ads sirf print ya scanned PDF mein rehte hain, searchable nahi hote.

RoznamaAds.pk isi gap ko fill karta hai — hum newspaper classifieds ko AI ke zariye extract kar ke, clean aur categorised format mein website par publish karte hain, taake koi bhi Pakistani in ads ko asaani se search aur browse kar sake.

Ye Kaam Kaise Hota Hai

1

Newspaper Scan/Collection

Jang, Express, Nawa-i-Waqt, Dawn jaisay bade newspapers ke classified pages collect kiye jate hain.

2

OCR + AI Extraction

Raw text se category, price, location aur contact details AI se automatically extract hoti hain.

3

Cleaning & Original Rewrite

Data clean kar ke naya, original wording generate hota hai — copy-paste nahi, taake copyright risk na ho.

4

Categorised Publish

Final listing Property, Jobs, Vehicles jaisi categories mein website par live hoti hai, searchable format mein.

Hum Kya Karte Hain

  • Multiple newspapers ke classifieds ek hi jagah aggregate karte hain
  • OCR aur AI se raw text ko clean, structured listings mein badalte hain
  • Property, Jobs, Vehicles, Matrimonial, Visa & Immigration, Auctions, Admissions, Tenders aur Government Notices jaisi categories cover karte hain
  • Users ko apni ad khud submit karne ki suhoolat dete hain
  • Bilingual (Urdu + English) searchable listings provide karte hain
  • CDA, RDA, BEOE ke official records se housing society aur visa agency verification dete hain
  • 9 tarah ke targets (society, job, phone number, website waghera) ke liye free Scam & Risk Checker chalate hain

Kyun Alag Hain

OLX, Zameen jaisi mainstream classified websites sirf user-submitted listings par based hain — wo newspaper ka data cover nahi karti. Bohot se log, khaas kar chhote shehron aur older generation, sirf newspaper mein hi apni ad dete hain — unka data online kahin available nahi hota. RoznamaAds.pk yehi untapped gap fill karta hai.

Isi tarah, Pakistan mein property aur overseas-job fraud bhi aam hai — aksar log society ya visa agency ka status confirm karne ke liye kisi single, free tool ki talash mein hote hain. Isi liye RoznamaAds.pk sirf ads tak mehdood nahi — Verification aur Scam Checker tools bhi isi platform ka core hissa hain.

Hamari Approach

Hum newspaper ka raw content copy nahi karte — AI se sirf structured data (category, price, location, contact) nikal ke naya, original wording generate karte hain. Isse copyright risk kam hota hai aur content bhi genuinely useful rehta hai.

Data Ki Reliability

Ads ke liye newspaper source aur admin-review dono layers hote hain. Verification/Scam Checker ke liye data sirf official government sources (BEOE, HEC, PPRA, CDA, RDA) se aata hai — koi unverified crowd-sourced data verification database mein shamil nahi hota.

Aage Kya Plan Hai

RoznamaAds.pk continuously naye newspapers add kar raha hai, aur Verification database ko bhi regular refresh kiya jata hai taake naye housing societies aur visa agencies bhi coverage mein aayein. Maqsad simple hai: Pakistan ke har shehar — chahe Karachi jitna bada ho ya Sargodha jaisa chhota — ke newspaper classifieds ko digital aur trust-worthy banana.

Kis Ke Liye Faidamand Hai

Roz-marra property/job/vehicle searchers, un logon ke ghar walay jo online kam comfortable hain lekin newspaper classifieds trust karte hain, overseas job seekers jo agency verify karna chahte hain, aur real estate agents jo apne clients ko sahi maloomat dena chahte hain — sab RoznamaAds.pk se faida uthate hain.
